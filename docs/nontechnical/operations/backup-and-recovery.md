# Backups and Recovery

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

We back up the documents, records that connect the document and signing systems, verification evidence, and the configuration needed to restore a customer environment. Each customer’s backup remains separate and encrypted.

We monitor backups and practice full restorations. The customer and business owners agree how much recent work could be lost in a major failure and how quickly service should be restored. After recovery, we check document access, document counts, signing history, and verification results before returning the system to normal use.
