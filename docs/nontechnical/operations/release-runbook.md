# Release Approval and Follow-Up

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

Every production release needs evidence that requirements, tests, security checks, operations readiness, and any licensing decisions are complete. When a release changes a commercial feature, privacy handling, or required attribution, the relevant business and legal owners must approve it.

After release, the team checks the service and watches it closely. Any issue with customer separation, document access, document integrity, or signature evidence is treated as a reason to roll back or start an incident response.
