# Starting a New Customer Environment

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

Every customer gets a separate environment with its own documents, databases, backups, security keys, and service accounts. Before documents are accepted, the team sets up the customer domain, access, storage, signing connection, backup schedule, monitoring, and document-verification settings.

We then test that people can sign in, upload and find documents, see only documents they are allowed to see, check signatures, send a signing request, and recover from a backup. Security, operations, the product owner, and the first customer administrator confirm the setup before handoff.
