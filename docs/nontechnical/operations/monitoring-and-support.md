# Monitoring and Customer Support

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

We watch for service outages, expiring security certificates, failed uploads, delayed processing, signing-notification problems, failed backups, low storage, suspicious access, and security vulnerabilities.

Support staff use only the access needed to solve a customer request and keep a record of what they did. They avoid putting documents, passwords, or signing links into support tickets. Possible data exposure, document tampering, or loss is handled through the security incident process.
