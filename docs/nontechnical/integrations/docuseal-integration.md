# How We Use DocuSeal

**Status:** Draft for stakeholder review

DocuSeal will run signing workflows: preparing a signing request, inviting people, tracking progress, and providing the completed PDF. It may also provide the technical signature checks used by the product.

Our product will communicate through approved DocuSeal interfaces and will not alter DocuSeal's private data. We will first confirm the exact DocuSeal version, the available interfaces, and which features require commercial terms.

Some features such as embedded or branded signing can have licensing requirements. We will not promise them until licensing and attribution obligations are reviewed and approved.
