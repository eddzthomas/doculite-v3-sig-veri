# What Happens If There Is a Security Problem

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

If we suspect unauthorized access, lost documents, stolen credentials, malware, or a defect in signature results, we record the event, contain it, investigate the facts, recover safely, and improve the system afterwards.

Serious events are led by a designated incident commander with security and operations support. We preserve evidence and avoid deleting information that could help understand what happened. Customer or regulatory notifications are decided by the authorized business and legal owners based on the facts.

The team practices response and recovery so that the plan is usable when it matters. This document describes the operational process; it does not define legal notification deadlines.
