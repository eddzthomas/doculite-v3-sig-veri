# How Information Is Handled and Kept

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

The system handles documents, document details, signing details, verification results, and limited operational records. We keep each category only for the purposes agreed with the customer: managing documents, checking signatures, sending signature requests, supporting the service, and recovering from failures.

Unsigned originals and completed signed documents remain separate so neither is silently replaced. When approved retention periods end, related product records are deleted and backup copies expire through the backup schedule. Support records should not contain unnecessary document contents or passwords.

Customers and legal/privacy reviewers decide retention lengths, legal holds, hosting regions, notices, and applicable privacy obligations. Engineering configures and verifies those decisions; it does not make legal determinations.
