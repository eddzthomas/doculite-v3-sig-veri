# Keeping Customer Documents Safe

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

Each customer receives their own separated system. Their documents, databases, backups, passwords, and security keys are not shared with another customer.

The document platform stores documents. The signing platform handles signing sessions. Our product connects them using their official interfaces; it does not reach directly into their private databases. Access is checked whenever someone views, downloads, verifies, or sends a document for signing.

We protect connections with encryption, keep sensitive keys out of browsers and logs, scan uploads for malware, limit who can administer the system, and monitor for failures or suspicious activity. The original document is kept intact for signature checks. A result such as “trusted signature” is kept separate from “created through DocuSeal.”

Before launch, security and operations leaders must approve the risk review, customer separation, backups, monitoring, and response procedures. This describes engineering safeguards; it is not a legal promise or legal advice.
