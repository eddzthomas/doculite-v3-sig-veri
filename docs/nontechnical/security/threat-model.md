# Risks We Plan For

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

We plan for the important ways a document system could fail or be misused: someone viewing a document they should not see, a customer seeing another customer’s data, stolen passwords, harmful uploads, fake signing notifications, altered PDFs, lost backups, or vulnerable third-party software.

For each risk, we assign a practical safeguard and a test. Examples include permission checks, separate customer environments, two-factor authentication for administrators, malware scanning, authenticated signing notifications, backup-restore drills, and regular dependency updates.

If a serious risk cannot be fixed before launch, a named security owner must document it, add a temporary safeguard, and get written approval. Decisions about legal validity of signatures or legal privacy duties belong to counsel and the customer’s policy owners.
