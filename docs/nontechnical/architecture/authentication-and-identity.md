# How People and Services Are Identified

**Status:** Draft for stakeholder review

People must sign in before they can see or act on a document. The product will check the document-management system's permission rules before showing document-related signing or verification information.

The final sign-in approach will be chosen before implementation. This document does not promise a particular login method or security feature before it has been selected and reviewed.

The application's connections to the document and signing services happen on the server. Their access credentials are not sent to a person's browser. External signers use the signing experience, not internal document-service credentials.
