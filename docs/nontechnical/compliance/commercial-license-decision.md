# Commercial Signing License Decision

**Audience:** Product sponsors, procurement, and non-technical reviewers

Before we offer embedded signing, white-label screens, template building, or other potentially paid DocuSeal features, we need written confirmation that the chosen plan permits them.

The decision will cover what is included, how branding and attribution work, price and renewal, usage limits, support, and how customer data is handled. If suitable terms are not available, the product must use an approved reduced experience or another signing option.

Product, procurement, counsel, security/privacy, and engineering must all approve before a commercially gated feature is turned on.
