# Legal and Licensing Decisions

**Audience:** Product sponsors, procurement, and non-technical reviewers

Some choices in this project are not engineering choices — they are legal or commercial ones, and they need a named person to make them in writing before we build on them. Examples: whether our DocuSeal plan permits the signing screens we want, what copyright notices we must show for the open-source software we deploy, and what we are allowed to tell customers about what a signature check means.

The legal decision register is one list that tracks all of these questions: what must be decided, who owns the decision, what written evidence is needed, and where the decision stands.

The rules are simple:

- Every question on the list has one accountable owner.
- A question is either open or answered with written evidence; nothing in between counts.
- Features that depend on an unanswered question stay switched off until that row is approved.
- The list records facts and approval states. Legal conclusions come from counsel, not from engineers or from this document.

When a new legal or licensing question appears, it gets a row on the list and, if it could hurt delivery, an entry in the risk register as well. Rows are never deleted — answered questions stay visible so future releases can show the evidence still holds.
