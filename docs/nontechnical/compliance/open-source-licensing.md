# Open-Source Software and Licensing

**Audience:** Product sponsors, customers, and non-technical reviewers

The product is built around two open-source systems: Paperless-ngx for document management and DocuSeal for signing. We operate them as separate services and build our own interface around their official APIs. We keep a record of the exact versions, licenses, notices, and changes we use.

Some open-source licenses may require notices, attribution, or making certain source changes available. Commercial signing features may also require a paid agreement. We will not assume those obligations away or copy paid features to avoid them.

Engineering collects the facts and maintains records. Qualified counsel must approve the final licensing, attribution, trademark, and source-availability approach before pilot and production.
