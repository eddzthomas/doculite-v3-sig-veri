# Product Requirements: Plain-Language Version

The first release must let the right people:

- Upload documents and see when they are ready.
- Search, organize, preview, and download documents they are allowed to access.
- See a clear signature result: no signature, valid and trusted, valid but not trusted, invalid, or unable to check.
- Ask for a fresh signature check.
- Send a document to people for signing and follow its progress.
- Receive the signed PDF back as a related document.

The system will protect access using Paperless permissions. It will keep customer data separated, never put service passwords in the browser, and handle retried background work safely. It will not claim to provide regulated, qualified, or long-term signature validation in this first release.
