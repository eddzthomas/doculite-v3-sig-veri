# User Journeys: Plain-Language Version

**Managing documents:** A team member uploads a file. The system processes it, makes it searchable, and checks its signature in the background. The person can then find it, read it, and download it if they have permission.

**Checking signatures:** On a document page, a person sees a short explanation of its current signature status. They can open more detail or ask the system to check again. “No signature” is shown as different from “invalid signature.”

**Getting signatures:** A team member chooses a document, sets up who needs to sign, and follows its progress. Signers use a separate secure signing page. When signing finishes, the final PDF is brought back as a new linked record; the original remains unchanged.

**Running the service:** Administrators manage access. Operators keep each customer’s separate system healthy, backed up, and recoverable.
